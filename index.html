An Avalanche Cross-Chain Bridge moves ETH, USDC and other tokens between Ethereum and the Avalanche C-Chain, and onward to Avalanche subnets, so assets can work wherever the liquidity is. How an Avalanche Cross-Chain Bridge works

You lock or burn assets on the source chain and receive them on the destination. An Avalanche Cross-Chain Bridge routes your transfer between Ethereum and the C-Chain, shows the amount that will arrive and the fee, then settles once the source transaction confirms. Reaching a subnet adds one more hop handled by the same flow.

Fees and time on the Avalanche Cross-Chain Bridge

Cost is the source-chain gas plus any protocol fee; the C-Chain side is cheap because AVAX gas is low. Timing depends on Ethereum confirmations and load, usually minutes. The quote shows the net amount received so you can compare routes before committing.

What you can move with an Avalanche Cross-Chain Bridge

ETH, USDC, USDT, WBTC and other liquid assets move both ways and then work across Avalanche DeFi — DEXs, lending and subnet apps. Availability depends on supported routes at the time; the interface lists reachable assets and the expected output for each.

Is the Avalanche Cross-Chain Bridge safe?

Self-custody keeps your keys in your wallet. Confirm the destination token's contract address, the amount received and the exact approval you grant, prefer a limited approval, and keep a little AVAX for gas. A seed-phrase request is always fraud.

Native route vs third-party cross-chain bridges

Native routes move major assets directly; third-party bridges reach more chains and assets but add a contract and a fee. Judge every route by the net amount received after gas and fees, and prefer audited routes with deep liquidity.
Do I need AVAX for an Avalanche Cross-Chain Bridge?
You pay source-chain gas to send, and you need a little AVAX on the C-Chain to move assets once they arrive.
How long does a cross-chain transfer to Avalanche take?
Usually minutes, after your source transaction gets enough confirmations; busy periods take longer.
Can I bridge back from Avalanche to Ethereum?
Yes, the route works both ways; you pay C-Chain gas in AVAX to send and Ethereum gas to receive.
Do I need an account?
No. You connect a self-custody wallet and bridge on-chain, with no sign-up and no custody.
What if a transfer stays pending?
Pending transfers usually clear once the network is less busy; a reverted source transaction leaves your assets intact minus gas.
